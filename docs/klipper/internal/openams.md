## OpenAMS Related Commands

These commands are used to manage OpenAMS units, including the `AFC_OpenAMS` unit driver (stuck spool recovery,
clog detection, and calibration) and the underlying `AFC_OAMS` hardware controller (spool load/unload, follower,
PID tuning, and retry status).
